FAQ

How to use Sprig, in plain language. Named after the screens and buttons in the app.

Getting started

What do I see when I open Sprig?
After you Agree and continue, the main tabs are Banking, Reports, and Settings. Portfolio appears once you have an investment account. A short tour walks the sample data the first time; Replay tour is in Settings if you want it again.
How do I connect my first bank?
Open Settings → Accounts → Add an account. Sign in on your bank’s own screen. Sprig then sets up your accounts (a short wait is normal) and Banking fills in automatically.
Which devices does Sprig run on?
iPhone and iPad. The app uses a tab layout on iPhone and a sidebar layout on iPad.
How do I get support?
Use the contact form. We reply there.

Accounts and banks

Where does the bank data come from?
You link each bank through Add an account. Links are read-only — money cannot move in or out through Sprig. Accounts update automatically after you connect; you do not pull to refresh.
How do I add another account?
Settings → Accounts → Add an account. Use the same flow for checking, savings, cards, loans, and brokerages your bank offers in that sign-in.
Can I add a manual account or a property account?
Not in the current app. Accounts come from a bank link. You can add manual transactions on a cash account, but there is no separate manual-account or property-account screen yet.
What does Treat as do?
On each account row in Settings → Accounts, Treat as lets you override Automatic with Cash, Credit, or Investment when the bank’s label is wrong. That changes how Sprig groups the account on Banking vs Portfolio.
How do I rename a bank or account?
In Settings → Accounts, tap the pencil next to the bank or account name, edit Name, then Save.
What does Disconnect do?
Disconnect stops syncing that bank and removes its accounts, transactions, and history from Sprig on this device.

Transactions

Can I enter my own transactions?
Yes. Tap the green + on Banking (or + inside an account register) to open Add transaction. Pick a checking, savings, or card account, then enter the merchant, Expense or Income, amount, date, category, optional tags, and note.
How does matching work?
When you log a purchase by hand, Sprig keeps it until the bank posts the same spend. It matches on exact amount, a close date, and a similar merchant name, then reconciles them so nothing is counted twice.
How do scheduled transactions work?
Open Settings → Recurring. Add rent, payroll, or a subscription with +, or adopt something under Found by Sprig. Scheduled items project forward on the register; you can pause, edit, or delete them there.
Can I import transactions from a CSV?
Yes. Settings → Import from CSV. Download the template if you need it, Choose CSV file, review the preview, then Confirm Import. Rows become manual transactions on the account you pick.
How do I change a category, merchant name, or tag?
Open the transaction in its register. Categories, Merchants, and Tags are also managed under Settings so defaults and labels stay consistent across accounts.

Budgets

How does budgeting work in Sprig?
There is no separate Budgets screen yet. Use Reports for income and spending by category, period, account, and tag. Use Safe to Spend and Set-asides on each cash account for what you can spend before the next paycheck.
How do I use Safe to Spend?
Open a cash account register. Safe to Spend is figured from your balance, pending and uncleared items, upcoming bills, and anything you Set aside. Tap How it’s figured for the line-by-line walk.
What are Set-asides?
From the register, open Set-asides and Add set-aside (for example an emergency cushion). That lowers Safe to Spend. Your bank balance does not change.
How are investments and net worth tracked?
Brokerages show under Portfolio: Portfolio Value, day change, allocation, and holdings per account. Reports also shows a net worth card with Cash and Invested. Banking’s Total Balance is spendable cash accounts, not full net worth.
What does Reports show?
Income, spending, spending by category, a monthly trend, top merchants, and net worth. Filter by period (This month, Last month, 3 months, 6 months, Year to date, All), category, tag, and account. Transfers between your own accounts are excluded unless you filter to them.

Settings

What does Theme do?
Under Appearance, Theme switches System, Light, or Dark for the whole app.
What is Accounts in Settings?
The list of linked banks. Add an account, rename, Treat as, or Disconnect from here.
What is Recurring?
Your Scheduled bills and paychecks, plus Found by Sprig suggestions you can Add, Edit, or Dismiss.
What are Merchants?
Saved merchant names and their default categories, so Add transaction and bank rows categorize faster.
What are Categories?
Built-in groups and subcategories (Income is separate). Expand a group to browse them, or add a custom subcategory with +.
What are Tags?
Optional labels you attach to transactions. They also appear as a filter on Reports once you have any.
What is Import from CSV?
Brings rows from a spreadsheet into an account as manual transactions after a preview and Confirm Import.
What is Low balance warning?
Under Alerts, turn it on and pick a Below amount ($100–$5,000). Sprig warns when a forecast dips under that line.
What does Replay tour do?
Under About, it runs the first-run tour again over the main screens.
How do I delete my Sprig account?
Settings → Delete Sprig account. That removes bank connections and your data on this device and cannot be undone.

Privacy

Where is my data stored?
On your device. Sprig never stores your financial data on its servers. Settings repeats that line at the bottom of the screen.
Are bank links read-only?
Yes. Onboarding requires you to acknowledge read-only access before you continue. Sprig cannot move money.
How does sync across iPhone, iPad, and Mac work?
Each device keeps its own local copy today. There is no in-app iCloud data sync and no Mac build in the current app. Use the same Apple ID subscription on iPhone and iPad; reconnect banks on a new device after install.
Where are Privacy Policy and Terms?
Settings → About links to Privacy Policy and Terms of Service on heysprig.com. They are also in the site footer.
