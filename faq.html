FAQ

Straight answers for people comparing Sprig to what they use today.

How much does Sprig cost?
$79.99/year. For a limited time, your first year is $49.99. There is no lifetime plan. No ads. We don't sell your data.
What's included?
Accounts and net worth (including property), a running balance, logging purchases that match to the bank, Safe to Spend, scheduled transactions, budgets, and investments. iPhone, iPad and Mac. Web is coming soon.
Which devices does Sprig run on?
iPhone, iPad, Mac. Web is coming soon.
How do bank connections work?
Bank links are read-only. No one can move your money through Sprig.
Where are my transactions stored?
We don't keep your financial transactions on our servers. They're stored on your Apple devices and in your own iCloud.
What happens when I log a purchase before the bank posts it?
Record it when you spend. When the bank catches up, Sprig matches the two so nothing is counted twice.
What is Safe to Spend?
One number for what you can spend today, plus a heads-up before an account runs low.
Does Sprig show upcoming bills and paychecks?
Yes. Scheduled transactions are laid out ahead of time so you see what's coming before it hits.
Can I budget in Sprig?
Yes. Plan by group and category, see what's left, and track spending, income and cash flow.
Does Sprig include investments and property?
Yes. Brokerage holdings sit next to everyday accounts, with net worth up top. Property is part of that view.
How do I cancel?
Cancel in your Apple Account subscription settings on your device. Apple handles billing and cancellation.
How do I get support?
Use the contact form. We reply there.
I'm on Copilot or Monarch. How do I switch?
See the switch offer for a discounted first year. Connect your accounts in Sprig, then cancel the old app when you're ready.
