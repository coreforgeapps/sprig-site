FAQ

How to use Sprig, named after the screens and buttons in the app.

Getting started

What are the main tabs?
Along the bottom you get Accounts, Insights, Budget, and Settings. On iPad, a sidebar lists Summary, Portfolio, and your banks, property, and loans.
How do I add a bank?
Tap + Add account (shown on Portfolio and elsewhere with the green +). Connecting a bank needs a subscription. Links are read-only — Sprig never trades or moves money.
Which devices does Sprig run on?
iPhone and iPad. The App Store gallery includes both. There are no Mac screenshots yet.
How do I get support?
Use the contact form. We reply there.

Accounts and banks

Where does the bank data come from?
You link each bank with + Add account and sign in through the bank flow. Accounts update on their own. Sprig is read-only — it never trades or moves your money.
How is Accounts organized?
On Accounts you can switch Net worth, Cash, Cards, Portfolio, Property, and Loans. Layout sits next to Settings in the top bar. Search and + are on the right.
Can I track property and loans?
Yes. Property shows items such as Car and Home. Loans shows items such as Mortgage. They sit in the Accounts list and in the iPad sidebar under Property and Loans.
What is Net worth?
Net worth is the Accounts view that totals everything. It shows Your total, a chart (1M, 6M, YTD, 1Y, All), then Cash, Cards, and the rest of your groups.
What is Balance vs Projected?
On a cash account (and under Cash on Accounts), Balance is what the bank shows now. Projected looks ahead using upcoming items on the register.

Transactions

Can I enter my own transactions?
Yes. Tap the green + on an account register. Manual entries use a pencil icon next to the date (for example Lunch with Dan). Pending bank items use an hourglass.
How does matching work?
The register has an Unmatched control next to Upcoming. Use it when a hand-entered spend still needs to line up with a bank download so nothing is counted twice.
How do scheduled transactions work?
Upcoming on the register lists bills and paychecks ahead of Today, with categories and a running balance. That is where you see what is coming before it posts.
How do I use Safe to Spend?
Open a cash account such as Everyday Checking. Safe to Spend sits under Balance / Projected. Switch Projected to see the balance after upcoming bills on the list below.
What do Select and Search do on a register?
Select is in the register toolbar for multi-select. Search opens find on that account’s transactions.

Budgets

How does budgeting work?
Open the Budget tab. My budget shows UNDER PLAN or over, spent of planned (for example $6,951 of $8,069), and a progress bar. Tap Edit plan to change the plan. Use Month or Year, and All accounts to filter.
How do categories work in Budget?
Under Categories you see each line with spent of planned and On plan, under, or over. Switch Groups, or tap See all / Show less. Open a category for more detail.
Can I see the year month by month?
Yes. On My budget choose Year, then Year to date, Full year, or Last 12 months. Spent this year and By month show each month under or over plan.
What is Insights?
Insights has Spending, Income, and Cash Flow. Cash flow is income minus spending, with a Summary for the period (for example Last month). Spending can show Category, Group, or Merchant, plus Spending mix and Top merchants.
How are investments tracked?
Open Portfolio (Accounts chip or iPad sidebar). See value, History or Holdings, allocation (ETFs, mutual funds, cash, stocks), accounts, and largest holdings. Footer copy: Read-only. Sprig never trades or moves money.

Settings

Where is Settings?
Settings is a main tab on iPhone and iPad (gear in the top bar on Accounts screens too). The App Store screenshots do not show the Settings list itself, so individual options are held until we can read build 402.
What is Layout?
Layout sits next to the gear on Accounts. Use it when you want to rearrange how Accounts is presented.

Privacy

Can Sprig move my money?
No. Portfolio states Read-only. Sprig never trades or moves money. Bank links are for viewing only.
Where is my data stored?
See the Privacy Policy. The site states financial data stays on your Apple devices and in your own iCloud — that wording is from the policy pages, not from an in-app Settings label in the screenshots.
Where are Privacy Policy and Terms?
In the site footer, and linked from Privacy and Terms.
